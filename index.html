01 / The Problem

Campus food is breaking
students' budgets

Every day, thousands of UTP students face an impossible choice: pay overpriced cafeteria food, eat poorly, or skip meals altogether. This is not a small inconvenience — it is a structural problem that directly affects academic performance and well-being.

The options available on campus are limited, overpriced, and often nutritionally inadequate. Students traveling long distances have no affordable alternative nearby. The result? Hunger during class, poor concentration, and growing frustration.

The Reality
02 / How Students Feel
Student Voices

Frustration.
Stress. Unfairness.

Students aren't just hungry — they're angry. Paying a large portion of their daily budget on a single mediocre meal feels disrespectful. Many students depend on financial aid or family support that barely covers tuition, let alone overpriced lunches.

03 / Evidence

Students are
speaking up online

From Instagram posts to Twitter threads, UTP students have been documenting and sharing their experiences with campus food for years. The complaints are consistent: high prices, poor quality, and zero accountability.
